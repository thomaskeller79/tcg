Leyline debug hub

Load a scenario, then open each seat in its own tab. Every tab polls the same live match.

  • Champion A's view — what A sees and can do.
  • Champion B's view — what B sees and can do.
  • True state — everything, unredacted (read-only, debug only).

How to play

  • Select a token on the board or a card in your hand; its legal abilities appear on the right.
  • Activate: pick an ability, then each target in order (highlighted on the board or as buttons), then Confirm. Abort any time before confirming — nothing is paid until then.
  • Everything you do enters Pending as a trace. It resolves when both Champions pass in a row. Respond with Quick / Reactive / Instant cards and abilities while you hold priority.
  • Defend by answering an Attack in Pending (buttons on the attack, or select a creature → Defend).
  • Your Action phase ends when you pass with Pending empty and the opponent passes too.
  • Auto-pass passes for you when Pass is your only legal action (never to end your own Action phase).
  • The Root view shows the hidden Slice; you only see what your own Root presence reveals.
