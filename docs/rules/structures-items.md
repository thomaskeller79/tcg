# Structures & Items

*Two non-actor Play-permanent card types (D17): **Structure** (stationary, hex-bound) and **Item** (carryable). Both lack an AP pool of their own, so — unlike the three actors (Creature/Champion/Companion) — someone else must fund and control their abilities, via the general ownership tree (`ownership.md`).*

**Decisions:** D24, D25, D31–D35 (`history/decisions.md`)

---

## Shape & location: Creature vs. Structure vs. Item

The three Play-permanent types are distinguished by which of **Attack / AP / Life** they carry, plus where they sit — this has nothing to do with **color** (mana-cost color pips, a separate, not-yet-designed axis, PLAN §8 Track A item 2). A colorless Creature is still simply a Creature. "Artifact" doesn't reintroduce a fourth type either — it dissolves per D17 (equippable → Item, stationary → Structure); a mechanically-flavored *Creature* (MTG's "Artifact Creature") stays `Type: Creature`, distinguished only by an optional subtype tag (the same mechanism Structure already uses for e.g. *Building*), never by becoming a Structure/Item.

| | Attack | AP | Life | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Creature** | yes | yes | yes | **(terrain, layer)** — Ground/Above/Below, D12; capacity 3 per layer |
| **Structure** | no by default (a printed ability may still let it deal damage — card-level data, not a base stat, D24) | no (D24 — funded like a spell, never self-paid) | yes (D24 — attackable, 0 Life → grave/ruins) | **(terrain, layer)** — its own dedicated slot, capacity **1 per layer** (D31) |
| **Item** | no | no | **no** (D34 — never a combat participant, so no damage-based destruction; removed only by an explicit printed effect) | **(terrain, layer)** while loose (D32), capacity-exempt/unbounded (D34) — or **its carrying actor** (Creature, Champion, or Companion) while equipped, not spatial at all (D26) |

### Location is uniformly (terrain, layer) — D32
A permanent's board location is the same shape everywhere a permanent has one at all: a **terrain cell plus a layer**. This was already true for Creature (D12); **D31** extends it to Structure (below) and **D32** extends it to a **loose** Item, closing the earlier open question of where a dropped item lives — it lives exactly where it was dropped, layer included (an underground creature's dropped sword is at (that cell, Below), hidden by the same rule that already hides Below creatures; a flyer's dropped item likewise lands wherever the item's own nature puts it, card by card). An **equipped** Item has no spatial location at all — its location *is* its carrying actor (D26).

**Legal layer(s) at cast time come from the card's own data/keywords, never a free choice by default (D32):** in the overwhelming majority of cases exactly one layer is legal — no keyword → Ground, Flying → Above, a burrower-type keyword → Below — matching the pessimistic-default posture (D14). A card may explicitly print a choice among multiple legal layers as opt-in flexibility (e.g. an amphibious creature choosing Ground or Below), but there is no generic "pick any layer" capability. Applies identically to Creature and Structure casts, and to placing a Structure at cast time (below).

### Casting/materialization procedure (D33)
Casting a Creature, Structure, or **Item** all follow the same procedure — no special case for Item: choose the spell → choose target terrain + legal layer → pay cost → the spell goes into the **Aether** (a trace ahead of the stack's `now` marker, `interaction-stack.md`, D16) → when `now` reaches it, it resolves and the permanent materializes at the chosen (terrain, layer). Because the board can change while a spell sits in the Aether (opponent responses, contested terrain, a layer filling up), resolution re-checks legality: **if the chosen target is no longer legal, that instruction fizzles rather than the whole spell being countered MTG-style** — for today's single-target permanent casts this looks identical in outcome to a counter, but the principle is general (a future multi-target spell only loses the parts that went illegal). Paid cost is never refunded either way. Full detail and rationale: `interaction-stack.md`.

## Structure

**Concept:** a stationary Play-permanent that lives on a terrain cell, on a layer, in its own dedicated slot — never in a creature's Ground/Above/Below room. (Visually: centered on the hex, layers arranged around it.)

- **Occupancy (D24, capacity extended D31):** one dedicated **Structure slot per (terrain, layer)**, capacity 1 per layer — not a creature layer slot, doesn't consume Ground/Above/Below creature capacity. A hex can hold 3 ground + 3 flying + 3 submerged creatures *and* up to **three** Structures (one per layer) simultaneously. A Below-layer Structure is hidden by default, for free, via the same rule that already hides Below creatures (D12/pillar 6) — this is how "a structure hidden underwater/inside a building" is achieved, without a separate containment mechanism.
- **Cast like a Creature (D20 pattern):** summoned onto a **bonded** terrain cell within the caster's realm, at whichever layer the card legally permits (usually exactly one, D32), that has a free Structure slot on that layer, paid in mana from the **shared** pool (Structures are cast through the Aether like any Spell — a Companion's private mana can never fund a cast, D22, only its own abilities).
- **Attackable, has Life:** follows the same pattern as a Creature/Champion (D14/D16) — 0 Life → destroyed → becomes a **grave/ruins in Play** (no slot consumed, same as a dead creature). "Indestructible" is a positive keyword, not a default.
- **Movement/LOS:** occupying a Structure slot has **no default effect** on movement or Line of Sight — a Structure doesn't block anything by default (it doesn't even compete for room). A specific card (e.g. a *Building* subtype) may print that it blocks LOS or movement, as cell-property data (pillar 5), not a base rule.
- **Payment and controller (`ownership.md`):** a Structure's parent is the **terrain cell it occupies**; that terrain's own parent is whoever currently bonds it (when bonded-active). Its ability is **funded** (payment walk) by that bonder directly, two hops up — which can be a Companion. Its **controller** (the strict, always-Champion walk) is that bonder's own Champion — so a Companion-bonded Structure is funded from the Companion's private pool but controlled by the Champion who cast that Companion. **This holds true even if a creature's own ability built the Structure** — the one-time build cost is paid by whoever recursively funds that creature (D27's "creature is always a leaf" rule), but the Structure's ongoing parent is always its terrain, never the builder. If the terrain becomes **unbonded or merely interrupted** (D27's three-state model — a temporary enemy-on-path pause counts too, not just a full unbond), the chain breaks — the Structure has neither payer nor controller until the interruption clears or someone (re-)bonds that cell.
  - **Emergent consequence, intentional:** because the terrain's bond state is tracked live, contested terrain (interrupted by an enemy on its path, or newly-unbonded after the Companion that bonded it dies) can be **re-bonded by the opposing side**, capturing the Structure standing on it — positionally, without ever attacking the Structure itself. Fits the existing "denial is positional and reversible" shape of D8; a Structure's vulnerability is different from a creature's (starve the land under it, don't fight it).
  - **Funding:** paid from the bonder's own pool (shared if Champion-bonded, that Companion's private pool if Companion-bonded). **Default cost shape is mana-only**, activated "like casting a spell" — mirroring Terrain exactly, since a Structure has no AP of its own either. A specific printed ability **may** additionally cost the funding actor's own AP (pillar 5, card-level data), same as any ability costing `mana + AP` (`overview.md` §4) — not a base-rule requirement. This same allowance applies to Terrain's own abilities too (`resources-terrain.md`).
  - **This describes an *activated* Structure ability specifically.** A Structure can equally carry a **triggered** ability (D35, `interaction-stack.md`) — "whenever a creature enters within distance N, deal 2 damage," no activation, no upfront cost — which only needs a **controller** (to know who it targets), not a payer, unless the trigger itself prints an optional resolution-time cost ("...you may pay 2 mana; if you do..."), which then follows the same funding walk as above.

## Item

**Concept:** a carryable Play-permanent — picked up and wielded by an actor (Creature, Champion, or Companion), rather than rooted to a hex like a Structure.

- **Location, while loose:** a (terrain, layer) address, same shape as everything else (D32) — and **capacity-exempt**: a loose Item never competes with the 3-creature-per-layer cap, and any number of loose Items may share a (terrain, layer) address (D34; exact board visualization for a pile of loose items is a UI question, not a rules one).
- **No Life (D34):** an Item is never a combat participant — no Attack, no AP, never a legal attack/ranged target the way a Structure or creature is — so a Life total would only matter for a hypothetical generic-damage/AoE destruction that nothing currently needs. An Item is removed from play only via an explicit printed effect (e.g. "destroy target Item," a sacrifice cost), never by combat damage.
- **Cast the same way as a Creature or Structure (D33):** target terrain + legal layer chosen at cast time, paid, goes into the Aether, materializes loose at that (terrain, layer) on resolution — no special case.
- **Equip is a generic actor ability, not a bespoke Item mechanic (D26):** every eligible actor holds `2AP: Equip target Item sharing this location` (cost is a tuning baseline, not locked). A card can strip this ability from a creature that shouldn't carry items (a beast can't wield a sword), or reprice it up/down per creature — ordinary D10 ability-replacement, nothing Item-specific.
- **An unequipped (loose) Item has no usable ability at all.** This is what keeps the ownership model a clean tree instead of needing a multi-parent/DAG case for "who can activate a loose item lying on a contested hex" — there's simply nothing to activate until someone equips it.
- **Funding, once equipped:** an Item's parent is its equipping actor — its ability is paid from that actor's own AP + whatever mana pool that actor's own chain resolves to (D26). Since exactly one actor can be an Item's parent at a time, **exclusivity is automatic** — no separate rule needed for "only one carrier." While equipped, an Item has no spatial (terrain, layer) location at all — it travels with its carrier implicitly.
- **"Stealing" an item is just Equip, costed:** an enemy actor standing on the same hex (and layer) as a loose Item can equip it via the same generic ability — a real, costed decision, not an ambient side-effect of proximity.
- **A specific Item can print an equip surcharge** ("when this is equipped, pay an additional 3 mana and 2 AP") — the same triggered-cost pattern already used for a non-basic terrain's bond surcharge (`card-ideas.md`), not a new mechanism.
- **When its carrier dies, an Item becomes ownerless (confirmed, D27):** its parent is removed and it reverts to loose **at the carrier's current (terrain, layer)**, re-equippable by anyone via the same generic ability. Unlike a Creature whose payer dying just orphans it in place (D26), an Item's whole point is that it *can* change hands.
- **Un-equip is a generic default ability too (D30):** `0AP`, free and uncapped — drops the Item to loose at the actor's current (terrain, layer). Same removable/re-priceable treatment as Equip (D10). Voluntary drop and carrier-death (above) now both resolve through the same "reverts to loose" outcome.

## Invariant vs. mutable
- **Invariant:** Structure and Item remain **non-actors** — neither has its own AP pool; every actor still has exactly the two resources (mana + AP, `economy.md`). Both resolve funding via the general ownership tree (D26): Structure's parent is its terrain; an Item's parent is whoever equips it (or nothing, while loose). Every spatial permanent's location is (terrain, layer) (D32); Item has no Life (D34); Structure capacity is 1 per (terrain, layer) (D31).
- **Mutable (card-driven):** whether a specific ability costs AP in addition to mana; whether a Structure blocks movement/LOS; whether a creature even has the Equip ability, and at what cost; which layer(s) a card may legally be cast onto; Item/Structure stats, Life (Structure only), and printed abilities.
