# Structures & Items

*Two non-Actor permanent card types (D17): **Structure** (stationary, hex-bound) and **Item** (carryable) — both **Objects** in the D44 terminology. Neither has *mana* access of its own, so — unlike the three **Actors** (Creature/Champion/Companion) — someone else must fund and control the mana portion of their abilities, via the general ownership tree (`ownership.md`). A Structure does hold its own **Activation Points** (D58), self-funding the AP portion directly; an Item holds none at all.*

**Decisions:** D24, D25, D31–D35, D39, D40, D41, D44, D46, D47, D54, D57, D58, D68, D70 (`history/decisions.md`)

---

## Shape & location: Creature vs. Structure vs. Item

Creature, Structure and Item are distinguished by which of **Attack / AP / Life** they carry, plus where they sit — this has nothing to do with **color** (mana-cost color pips, a separate, not-yet-designed axis, PLAN §8 Track A item 2). A colorless Creature is still simply a Creature. "Artifact" doesn't reintroduce a fourth type either — it dissolves per D17 (equippable → Item, stationary → Structure); a mechanically-flavored *Creature* (MTG's "Artifact Creature") stays `Type: Creature`, distinguished only by an optional subtype tag (the same mechanism Structure already uses for e.g. *Building*), never by becoming a Structure/Item.

| | Attack | AP | Life | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Creature** | yes | yes | yes | **(terrain, level)** — Surface/Air/Underground, D12/D41; capacity 3 per level |
| **Structure** | no by default (a printed ability may still let it deal damage — card-level data, not a base stat, D24) | yes — its own printed **Activation Points** (D58), self-funding its own printed ability's AP portion directly, no climbing | yes (D24 — attackable, 0 Life → **Ruin**, which occupies its slot, D40) | **(terrain, level)** — its own dedicated slot, **two per hex**: a shared Surface slot (Ground+Air) and a separate Underground slot (D24, D40) |
| **Item** | no | no | **no** (D34 — never a combat participant, so no damage-based destruction; removed only by an explicit printed effect) | **(terrain, level)** while loose (D32), capacity-exempt/unbounded (D34) — or **its carrying actor** (Creature, Champion, or Companion) while equipped, not spatial at all (D26) |

### Location is uniformly (terrain, level) — D32
A permanent's board location is the same shape everywhere a permanent has one at all: a **terrain cell plus a level**. True for Creature (D12), Structure (D31/D40), and a **loose** Item (D32) alike — a loose Item lives exactly where it was dropped, level included (an Underground creature's dropped sword is at (that cell, Underground), hidden by the same rule that already hides Underground creatures; a flyer's dropped item likewise lands wherever the item's own nature puts it, card by card). An **equipped** Item has no spatial location at all — its location *is* its carrying actor (D26).

**Legal level(s) at cast time come from the card's own data/keywords, never a free choice by default (D32):** in the overwhelming majority of cases exactly one level is legal — no keyword → Surface, Flying → Air, Subterranean → Underground (D42) — matching the pessimistic-default posture (D14). A card may explicitly print a choice among multiple legal levels as opt-in flexibility (e.g. an amphibious creature choosing Surface or Underground), but there is no generic "pick any level" capability. Applies identically to Creature and Structure casts, and to placing a Structure at cast time (below).

### Casting/materialization procedure (D33, D46, D70)
Casting a Creature, Structure, or **Item** all follow the same procedure — no special case for Item: choose the card → **pay its complete cost** (D70; necessary for X-costs and cost-scaled modal effects) → make any modal choices → **choose target terrain + legal level** (the permanent's one mandatory, implicit target, `object-properties.md` §4) — cost is paid, and modes chosen, *before* the target is chosen (D70; full procedure `interaction-stack.md`) — the card goes into the **Aether** (a trace in Pending, ahead of `Now`, `interaction-stack.md`, D16/D38) → when `Now` reaches it, it resolves: the Aether object stays put as a past trace (it does not relocate onto the Island) and its resolution **creates a separate, linked permanent** at the chosen (terrain, level) — two distinct objects, one per zone, not one object materializing across zones (D16). **A card is only ever offered as a legal play if at least one legal cost-and-target combination exists (D46)** — no paying into a target-less cast. Because the board can change while a card sits in the Aether (opponent responses, contested terrain, a slot filling up), resolution re-checks legality: **if the chosen target is no longer legal, that instruction fizzles rather than the whole card being countered MTG-style** — for today's single-target permanent casts this looks identical in outcome to a counter, but the principle is general (a future multi-target card only loses the parts that went illegal). Paid cost is never refunded either way. **A separate, earlier check (D68) can also fire at the initial instantiate step itself**, before anything reaches the Aether at all: if the chosen (terrain, level) turns out illegal against true state because of a concealed occupant the caster's belief state didn't show, the player redirects to a different eligible (terrain, level) or cancels, repeating until one succeeds — cost, including the card, is sunk regardless. Full detail and rationale: `interaction-stack.md`.

## Structure

**Concept:** a stationary permanent that lives on a terrain cell, on a level, in its own dedicated slot — never in a creature's Surface/Air/Underground room. (Visually: centered on the hex, levels arranged around it.)

- **Occupancy (D24, D40):** **two** dedicated Structure/Ruin slots per hex — a shared **Surface** slot (Ground+Air combined; Air isn't expected to be structure-dense enough to need its own) and a separate **Underground** slot (concealment kept as its own axis) — not creature-level slots, don't consume Surface/Air/Underground creature capacity. A hex can hold 3 Surface + 3 Air + 3 Underground creatures *and* up to **two** Structures (one Surface, one Underground) simultaneously. An Underground Structure is hidden by default, for free, via the same rule that already hides Underground creatures (D12/pillar 6) — this is how "a structure hidden underground/inside a building" is achieved, without a separate containment mechanism. **A destroyed Structure leaves a Ruin that occupies its slot** (unlike a Grave, which is always free, D40) — building a new Structure directly onto a ruined slot replaces the ruin in one action; an explicit effect can also clear a ruin directly.
- **Cast like a Creature (D20 pattern):** summoned onto a **bonded** terrain cell within the caster's realm, at whichever level the card legally permits (usually exactly one, D32), that has a free Structure/Ruin slot on that level, paid in mana from the **shared** pool (a Companion's private mana can never fund a cast, D22, only its own abilities). Cost is paid before the target/level choice is instantiated (D46).
- **Attackable, has Life:** follows the same pattern as a Creature/Champion (D14/D16) — 0 Life → destroyed → becomes a **Ruin** (occupies its slot, D40, unlike a dead creature's free Grave). "Indestructible" is a positive keyword, not a default.
- **Its own Activation Points (D58):** a Structure carries its own printed Activation Points, the same resource every non-Item permanent holds — it both self-gates "has this printed ability already fired this round" *and* directly funds the AP portion of that ability's cost. **A Structure is cast, so it's summoning-sick like a Creature (D57)** — it enters with 0 Activation Points, refreshing at its first eligible refresh, unlike Terrain (which is placed, not cast, and never subject to this at all). Haste removes this for a Structure exactly as it does for a Creature.
- **Movement/LOS:** occupying a Structure slot has **no default effect** on movement or Line of Sight — a Structure doesn't block anything by default (it doesn't even compete for room). A specific card (e.g. a *Building* subtype) may print that it blocks LOS or movement, as cell-property data (pillar 5), not a base rule.
- **Payment and controller (`ownership.md`):** a Structure's parent is the **terrain cell it occupies**; that terrain's own parent is whoever currently bonds it (when controlled). Its ability's **mana** portion is **funded** (payment walk) by that bonder, two hops up — which can be a Companion. Its ability's **Activation Points** portion is self-funded directly from the Structure's own printed Activation Points (D58) — it never climbs, same as a Creature's own AP never climbs. Its **controller** (the strict, root-of-the-chain walk) is that bonder's own Champion when the terrain is controlled, or **Neutral** when it isn't (D54) — so a Companion-bonded Structure has its mana funded from the Companion's private pool but is controlled by the Champion who cast that Companion. **This holds true even if a creature's own ability built the Structure** — the one-time build cost is paid by whoever recursively funds that creature (D27's "creature is always a leaf" rule), but the Structure's ongoing parent is always its terrain, never the builder. If the terrain's controller becomes **Neutral** (D54 — a temporary path-block counts, not just a full unbond), the Structure's mana funding disappears with it — but its own Activation Points keep working regardless, since they were never routed through the terrain to begin with. See `neutral-permanents.md`: this is exactly what lets a Neutral Structure with an Activation-Points-only ability act entirely on its own via a Behavior, with no bonder at all.
  - **Emergent consequence, intentional:** because the terrain's controller status is tracked live, contested terrain (its path blocked by an enemy, or newly-unbonded after the Companion that bonded it dies) can be **re-bonded by the opposing side**, capturing the Structure's mana funding standing on it — positionally, without ever attacking the Structure itself. Fits the existing "denial is positional and reversible" shape of D8; a Structure's vulnerability is different from a creature's (starve the land under it, don't fight it).
  - **Default cost shape is mana-only**, activated like playing any card — mirroring Terrain. A specific printed ability **may** additionally cost the Structure's own Activation Points (pillar 5, card-level data), same as any ability costing `mana + AP` (`overview.md` §4) — not a base-rule requirement, but no longer requiring a live bonder either, since that portion is self-funded (D58). This same allowance applies to Terrain's own abilities too (`resources-terrain.md`).
  - **This describes an *activated* Structure ability specifically.** A Structure can equally carry a **triggered** ability (D35, `interaction-stack.md`) — "whenever a creature enters within distance N, deal 2 damage," no activation, no upfront cost — which only needs a **controller** (to know who it targets), not a payer, unless the trigger itself prints an optional resolution-time cost ("...you may pay 2 mana; if you do..."), which then follows the same funding walk as above.

## Item

**Concept:** a carryable permanent — picked up and wielded by an actor (Creature, Champion, or Companion), rather than rooted to a hex like a Structure.

- **Location, while loose:** a (terrain, level) address, same shape as everything else (D32) — and **capacity-exempt**: a loose Item never competes with the 3-creature-per-level cap, and any number of loose Items may share a (terrain, level) address (D34; exact board visualization for a pile of loose items is a UI question, not a rules one).
- **No Life (D34):** an Item is never a combat participant — no Attack, no AP, never a legal attack/ranged target the way a Structure or creature is — so a Life total would only matter for a hypothetical generic-damage/AoE destruction that nothing currently needs. An Item is removed from the Island only via an explicit printed effect (e.g. "destroy target Item," a sacrifice cost), never by combat damage.
- **Cast the same way as a Creature or Structure (D33, D46):** cost paid first, then target terrain + legal level chosen, goes into the Aether, materializes loose at that (terrain, level) on resolution — no special case.
- **Equip is a generic actor ability, not a bespoke Item mechanic (D26):** every eligible actor holds `2AP: Equip target Item sharing this location` (cost is a tuning baseline, not locked). A card can strip this ability from a creature that shouldn't carry items (a beast can't wield a sword), or reprice it up/down per creature — ordinary D10 ability-replacement, nothing Item-specific.
- **An unequipped (loose) Item has no usable ability at all.** This is what keeps the ownership model a clean tree instead of needing a multi-parent/DAG case for "who can activate a loose item lying on a contested hex" — there's simply nothing to activate until someone equips it. Unlike every other permanent, an Item never holds Activation Points (D58) — it's the one Object excluded from that resource entirely.
- **Funding, once equipped:** an Item's parent is its equipping actor — its ability is paid from that actor's own AP + whatever mana pool that actor's own chain resolves to (D26). Since exactly one actor can be an Item's parent at a time, **exclusivity is automatic** — no separate rule needed for "only one carrier." While equipped, an Item has no spatial (terrain, level) location at all — it travels with its carrier implicitly.
- **"Stealing" an item is just Equip, costed:** an enemy actor standing on the same hex (and level) as a loose Item can equip it via the same generic ability — a real, costed decision, not an ambient side-effect of proximity.
- **A specific Item can print an equip surcharge** ("when this is equipped, pay an additional 3 mana and 2 AP") — the same triggered-cost pattern already used for a non-basic terrain's bond surcharge (`card-ideas.md`), not a new mechanism.
- **When its carrier dies, an Item becomes ownerless (confirmed, D27):** its parent is removed and it reverts to loose **at the carrier's current (terrain, level)**, re-equippable by anyone via the same generic ability. Unlike a Creature whose payer dying just orphans it in place (D26), an Item's whole point is that it *can* change hands.
- **Un-equip is a generic default ability too (D30):** `0AP`, free and uncapped — drops the Item to loose at the actor's current (terrain, level). Same removable/re-priceable treatment as Equip (D10). Voluntary drop and carrier-death (above) now both resolve through the same "reverts to loose" outcome.

## Invariant vs. mutable
- **Invariant:** Structure and Item remain **non-Actors** (D44). Structure holds its own Activation Points like any non-Item permanent (D58) and self-funds its own AP costs; Item holds none. Mana funding still resolves via the general ownership tree (D26): Structure's parent is its terrain; an Item's parent is whoever equips it (or nothing, while loose). Every spatial permanent's location is (terrain, level) (D32); Item has no Life (D34); Structure/Ruin capacity is **two slots per hex** — Surface (Ground+Air) and Underground (D40); a Ruin occupies its slot, a Grave never does. A Structure is cast, so it's summoning-sick (D57) and can be granted a Behavior while Neutral (`neutral-permanents.md`) if its ability is self-payable.
- **Mutable (card-driven):** whether a specific ability costs AP in addition to mana; whether a Structure blocks movement/LOS; whether a creature even has the Equip ability, and at what cost; which level(s) a card may legally be cast onto; Item/Structure stats, Life (Structure only), and printed abilities.
